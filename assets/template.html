Analytical build 2.2

Seven inputs, fixed weights

Each signal is on a 0–100 scale. Multiply by its weight, add the contributions, then round the total to one decimal. Scores are priorities—not probabilities.

The weights and thresholds are inherited assumptions, not fitted parameters. No model runs in the browser.

What changed in this edition

Demo-data enrichment: Buying-stage scenarios across the portfolio provide a broader range of examples while preserving the scoring weights and thresholds. Original CSVs are retained; effective inputs and a scenario manifest are included in the package. These examples are illustrative, not evidence of predictive performance.

  1. Whitespace is preserved. Explicit “No Footprint” records no longer fall through to Cloud. Mixed Cloud + On-Premise is treated as Hybrid.
  2. Pipeline is counted at the right grain. Only open pipeline for each account and product is added. It is not repeated for every product an account has.
  3. Ranks and rows agree. Score, product pipeline, account ID and product name define a deterministic order.
  4. The full picture is visible. Explore all account–product scores or focus on strategic pursuits scoring 55+.

How the signals are calculated

Customer fit · 20%
Revenue (40%), employee count (30%) and target-industry membership (30%). These are account-level signals.
Customer intent · 20%
Account activity over a 365-day relative window: 70% normalized age-weighted activity + 30% normalized event count. It is shared across that account’s products.
Product engagement · 15%
A supplied synthetic 0–100 index. The upload does not contain product-level raw events to independently explain this index.
Whitespace · 10%
100 for No Footprint; otherwise 0. This is an investigation opportunity, not confirmed demand.
Modernization · 15%
On-Premise: 85 if engagement ≥60, otherwise 55. Hybrid: 65. Cloud: 20. No Footprint: 0.
Competition · 10%
Maximum recorded competitor intensity for the account and product. No record contributes zero; it does not prove no competition.
Product pipeline · 10%
Open product pipeline normalized against the full portfolio. Closed, won and lost records are excluded. USD is the inherited display assumption.

Interpretation & safeguards

Pursuit types: Whitespace first; otherwise Modernization if its signal is ≥65; otherwise Competitive if intensity is ≥60; otherwise Expansion. These are rule-based review categories, not confirmed buying plans.

Signal bands: Very Strong ≥85, Strong ≥70, Moderate ≥55, Emerging ≥40, Not Prioritized <40. The full demo contains examples across all bands. The Strategic view excludes scores below 55, and narrow filters may leave bands empty.

Known limitations: Min–max normalization depends on the source portfolio and can be sensitive to outliers. It is calculated once at build time, not recalculated when you filter. Engagement affects both intent and modernization indirectly; validate overlapping evidence before production.

Do not infer: A causal revenue lift, a win probability, new revenue from existing pipeline, or product-specific buyer intent from account-wide activity.

Production next step: Validate source freshness, missingness and event identity. Test ranking quality with time-separated outcomes and sales-user review before taking action.
